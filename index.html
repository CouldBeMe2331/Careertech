What to Expect

How It Works

🏆 Tournament Flow

The tournament uses a double elimination bracket — every player gets a second chance after their first loss. Winners advance through the winners bracket; those who lose drop to the losers bracket. Lose again in losers and you're eliminated. The final two players compete in Grand Finals.

If the Losers Bracket finalist wins Grand Finals, a bracket reset occurs and a second Best 3-out-of-5 set is played. The winners-side player has only lost once, so you must win both sets to claim the championship.

Tournament Rules
⚙ General Settings
  • Stocks: 3

  • Items: OFF
  • Stage Hazards: OFF
  • FS Meter: OFF
  • Spirits: OFF
  • Damage Handicap: OFF

  • Custom Balance: OFF
📋 Match Procedure
  • Players select characters (any non-banned character)
  • Players strike stages until one remains — or simply agree
  • Match is played on the remaining stage
  • Winner of previous game bans 2 stages
  • Loser picks from remaining legal stages
  • Winner may change character first, then loser responds

🚫 Banned Behaviors
  • Stalling: Intentionally making the game unplayable or excessively delaying the match
  • Pausing: Pausing during a match results in loss of a stock
  • Sportsmanship: Be kind to your opponent. T-bagging is only permitted against friends or when beating a beyo

If Time Runs Out

Tiebreaker Rules

  1. Player with the most stocks remaining wins
  2. If stocks are tied — player with the lowest damage percentage wins
  3. If both criteria are equal — a 1-stock 3-minute rematch is played
